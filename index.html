06 / FAQ

Common questions

How can we work together?

Fixed-scope projects or hourly work, whichever suits the job. Short consulting engagements are fine too. We agree scope and a first milestone before I start.

WordPress or a custom build?

WordPress suits content-led sites where your team edits pages themselves. A custom build suits sites with unusual interactions or tight performance needs. I'll recommend whichever fits, not whichever is easier for me.

How do you use AI on code work?

LLM pipelines handle the repetitive parts: analysis, draft refactors, tests and docs. Every change is reviewed by me and checked against your tests before it reaches your codebase.

How long does a project take?

It depends on scope. A small site or automation can take a couple of weeks; larger builds or migrations take longer. You get a realistic estimate after the scoping call.
